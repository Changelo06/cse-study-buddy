# CSE Ready: Project Overview

## 1. Executive Summary
CSE Ready is a review web application for the Philippine Civil Service Examination. It is intended for both Professional and Subprofessional examinees who need a more structured, trackable, and accessible way to prepare for the exam. The platform helps users study through organized lessons, topic-based modules, flashcards, timed quizzes, progress tracking, analytics, and a final CSE-style readiness exam. 

The main goal is to make review less random and more guided. Instead of relying only on scattered PDF reviewers or unstructured question lists, users can follow a course-like learning path, see what they have finished, understand where they are weak, and practice under time pressure.

**Important Ethical Boundary**: CSE Ready is not affiliated with the Civil Service Commission. It must never claim to provide actual Civil Service Examination questions. All practice items must be original CSE-style practice questions created for learning and review purposes only.

## 2. Product Vision
The long-term vision is to combine structured content, progress tracking, timed practice, and analytics into one learning hub (Coursera-style learning path).

**Long-term product direction:**
- Coursera-style learning paths for Professional and Subprofessional users
- Minimalist dashboard that acts as the user's central study hub
- Topic-based lessons with ordered modules
- Timed quizzes with A, B, C, D, E choices
- Flashcards for memory-heavy topics
- Analytics for strengths, weaknesses, speed, and readiness
- Final CSE Ready exam using original CSE-style practice questions
- Ethical AI-assisted drafting with human review and references
- Admin panel for uploading lessons, modules, quizzes, flashcards, and references

The ideal version of CSE Ready is a training system that helps users answer:
- *How ready am I?*
- *What should I study next?*
- *What topics am I weak in?*
- *Am I fast enough under timed pressure?*

## 3. Target Users
- First-time CSE takers
- Repeat takers
- Working students
- Fresh graduates
- Government job applicants
- Both Professional and Subprofessional examinees

## 4. Core Value Proposition
CSE Ready helps users prepare in a more organized and measurable way.
- Organized learning paths instead of random review files
- Progress tracking by lesson and module
- Readiness estimate to help users understand preparation quality
- Weakness-based study planning
- Timed quizzes to simulate exam pressure

## 5. Design Direction
Visually feel like a friendly learning dashboard inspired by course platforms, notebook layouts, and playful educational cards.

- **Design Style**: Playful but academic, grid-paper/notebook-inspired background, large rounded cards, clean layout with bright color coding.
- **Visual Identity**: Subtle Filipino identity with small Philippine-inspired color accents (blue, yellow, red, white). Avoid overly official or government-clone looks.
- **Typography**: 
  - `Fredoka` or similar rounded bold font for page titles, cards, nav labels.
  - `Comica` or similar soft rounded font for secondary playful text.
  - Clean sans-serif fallback for long reading sections.

## 6. Content Direction & User Integrity
- Lessons must be structured/reviewed by humans. AI can assist but requires review.
- References should prioritize official and credible sources (e.g., Constitution, RA 6713).
- **Anti-Cheating**: Lock answers until completion, timed quizzes, track retakes, show answer keys only after submission.

## 7. Monetization Strategy
- Keep the MVP free or mostly free to gain trust.
- Possible future pricing: Premium mock exams, printable packages, organization access.
